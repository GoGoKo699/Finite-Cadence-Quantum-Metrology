# Primary sources

[Related work](RELATED_WORK.md) · [Model and results](../science/model.md)

The identifiers below separate inherited tools from the physical model and global risk statements proved in this repository. Version-specific links are supplied where the comparison depends on a particular preprint. A preprint and a later published article are not interchangeable sources.

## HNLS18

Sisi Zhou, Mengzhen Zhang, John Preskill, and Liang Jiang, **Achieving the Heisenberg limit in quantum metrology using quantum error correction**, *Nature Communications* **9**, 78 (2018). [Publisher](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-017-02510-3) · [arXiv:1706.02445v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.02445v2).

Theorem 1 supplies the Hamiltonian-not-in-Lindblad-span criterion under fast, accurate controls. The infinitesimal correction assumption is material to the comparison.

## FAST17

Pavel Sekatski, Michalis Skotiniotis, Janek Kołodyński, and Wolfgang Dür, **Quantum metrology with full and fast quantum control**, *Quantum* **1**, 27 (2017). [Publisher](https://doi.org/10.22331/q-2017-09-06-27) · [arXiv:1603.08944v5](https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.08944v5).

Sections 4.1 and 5.2 and Appendix F cover error-corrected sensing and globally attainable inverse-square risk with charged sensing time in the fast-control model.

## ADAPT17

Rafał Demkowicz-Dobrzański, Jan Czajkowski, and Pavel Sekatski, **Adaptive Quantum Metrology under General Markovian Noise**, *Physical Review X* **7**, 041009 (2017). [Publisher](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevX.7.041009) · [arXiv:1704.06280v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/1704.06280v3).

Section IV treats cancellation at a reference parameter and first-order error-correction conditions. It is an antecedent for the local compensation mechanism.

## CHANNEL21

Sisi Zhou and Liang Jiang, **Asymptotic theory of quantum channel estimation**, *PRX Quantum* **2**, 010343 (2021). [Publisher](https://doi.org/10.1103/PRXQuantum.2.010343) · [arXiv:2003.10559v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.10559v3).

Theorems 1–3 supply the local Hamiltonian-not-in-Kraus-span classification. Sections IV–V construct a noiseless corrected tangent; Section III treats generalized dephasing.

## SUBSYSTEM26

Qiushi Liu and Sisi Zhou, **Subsystem Quantum Error Correction for Noisy Quantum Metrology**, [arXiv:2606.19628v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19628v1) (17 June 2026).

Equation (1), Theorem 2, and Appendix C.4 are the closest algebraic comparison: factorized Pauli-error channels, local QFI, and a sufficient condition outside the generated noise algebra. That algebraic sufficiency principle is prior work.

## ANCILLA25

Qiushi Liu and Yuxiang Yang, **Heisenberg-Limited Quantum Metrology without Ancillae**, *Physical Review Letters* **135**, 140801 (2025). [Publisher](https://doi.org/10.1103/vmd7-twd5) · [arXiv:2403.04585v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.04585v3).

Theorem 3 gives an earlier Pauli-channel construction whose surviving signal component must also commute with the error generators.

## FIXED21

Nathan Shettell, William J. Munro, Damian Markham, and Kae Nemoto, **Practical Limits of Error Correction for Quantum Metrology**, *New Journal of Physics* **23**, 043038 (2021). [Publisher](https://doi.org/10.1088/1367-2630/abf533) · [arXiv:2101.02823v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.02823v2).

Equation (6) contains the finite-cycle transverse-noise response used here, up to conventions. Section 4 discusses adaptive inverse fields; Appendix D concerns adaptive measurement bases.

## BIAS22

Ivan Rojkov, David Layden, Paola Cappellaro, Jonathan Home, and Florentin Reiter, **Bias in Error-Corrected Quantum Sensing**, *Physical Review Letters* **128**, 140503 (2022). [Publisher](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.128.140503) · [arXiv:2101.05817](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.05817) · [Author-hosted article](https://web.mit.edu/pcappell/www/pubs/Rojkov22.pdf).

Finite correction strength or frequency changes the calibrated signal response as well as the decoherence rate.

## INFORMATIVE

Hongzhen Chen, Yu Chen, Jing Liu, Zibo Miao, and Haidong Yuan, **Quantum Metrology Enhanced by Leveraging Informative Noise with Error Correction**, *Physical Review Letters* **133**, 190801 (2024). [Publisher](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.133.190801) · [Author-hosted main text](https://hzchen.me/files/papers/Chen2024prl-preprint.pdf).

The earlier source is **Yu Chen, Hongzhen Chen, Jing Liu, Zibo Miao, and Haidong Yuan**, **Fluctuation-enhanced quantum metrology**, [arXiv:2003.13010v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.13010v1) (2020). Its title and author order differ. The comparison uses published Eq. (12) and the earlier manuscript's Section IV, Eqs. (14)–(15), and Section VII; it does not assume access to the published supplement.

## GLOBAL22

Masahito Hayashi, Zi-Wen Liu, and Haidong Yuan, **Global Heisenberg scaling in noisy and practical phase estimation**, *Quantum Science and Technology* **7**, 025030 (2022). [Publisher](https://doi.org/10.1088/2058-9565/ac5d7e) · [arXiv:2110.01844v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.01844v1).

The phase-damping threshold is inverse in the number of uses. Global average-loss and limiting-distribution claims, including the single-qubit adaptive construction, have distinct scopes.

## RLD21

Vishal Katariya and Mark M. Wilde, **Geometric distinguishability measures limit quantum channel estimation and discrimination**, *Quantum Information Processing* **20**, 78 (2021). [Publisher](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11128-021-02992-7) · [arXiv:2004.10708v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.10708v2).

Propositions 29 and 38 provide the supported-Choi channel RLD expression and its chain rule used in the adaptive converse. These information inequalities are inherited tools.

## CSS26

Liaubaitė, Ramacciotti, and Raussendorf, **CSS codes for Quantum Metrology with Discrete-time Error Correction**, [arXiv:2609.40022v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40022v1) (2026).

Section III and the discussion analyze the finite Heisenberg-like window and SQL crossover for the specified CSS preparation, correction, and readout protocol. This is a protocol-specific finite-cadence comparison.

## MEMORY26

Shravan and Thurtell, **Heisenberg-limited metrology in the presence of non-Markovian noise with finite control rates**, [arXiv:2607.12913v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12913v1) (2026).

Theorem 3 and Eqs. (35)–(40) describe finite-control-rate approximations and their time windows in a non-Markovian system–bath setting. Instantaneous operations and the control interval are separate resources.

## BOSON26

Daniel G. Li, Lorcán O Conlon, Yu-Xin Wang, Erfan Abbasgholinejad, Twesh Upadhyaya, and Alexey V. Gorshkov, **Fault-Tolerant Heisenberg-Limited Quantum Metrology with Bosonic Probes**, [arXiv:2610.40405v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40405v1) (29 October 2026).

The finite-signal analysis and Appendices G.2, H.6, and I.2 discuss quadratic residual-signal diffusion, compensating drives, and the separate cost of global localization. The bosonic and circuit-noise resource model differs from the present ideal boundary-control model.

## AUTO26

Hyukgun Kwon, Uwe R. Fischer, Seung-Woo Lee, and Liang Jiang, **Restoring Heisenberg scaling in time via autonomous quantum error correction**, *npj Quantum Information* **12**, 103 (2026). [Publisher](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41534-026-01268-1) · [arXiv:2504.13168v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.13168v3).

Theorem 1 concerns engineered autonomous correction under its commuting-noise conditions. The correction strength in the error guarantee is chosen for the specified sensing horizon.

## INTERPOLATE17

Ajoy et al., **Quantum interpolation for high-resolution sensing**, *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences* **114**, 2149–2153 (2017). [Publisher](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1610835114).

Combining available propagators can synthesize finer effective controls. Therefore field spacing alone cannot supply a general precision floor; the noisy physical-channel converse must include allowed sequences of grid settings.

## FAULT26

Himanshu Sahu, Qian Xu, and Sisi Zhou, **Achieving the Heisenberg limit using fault-tolerant quantum error correction**, [arXiv:2601.05457v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.05457v2) (2026).

This addresses circuit-level bit-flip and SPAM errors with growing probe/code resources. The present fixed-dimensional result assumes ideal boundary operations and does not establish that fault-tolerance guarantee.

## NOISERATE

**Noise-Rate-Activated-Sensing**, public scientific repository, revision `e4c8a7d4f15c3b35dda1507caf7e411502b9b100`.

[Physical scope](https://github.com/GoGoKo699/Noise-Rate-Activated-Sensing/blob/e4c8a7d4f15c3b35dda1507caf7e411502b9b100/science/physical_scope.md) · [Recorded phase estimation](https://github.com/GoGoKo699/Noise-Rate-Activated-Sensing/blob/e4c8a7d4f15c3b35dda1507caf7e411502b9b100/science/recorded_phase_estimation.md).

The local compensated telegraph response and weighted nested-interval estimation are antecedents. Its microscopic global theorem holds physical compensation fixed and addresses a controlled-rate, unknown-axis device.

## BATHRESET

**Quantum-Sensing-Without-Bath-Resets**, public scientific repository, revision `b32469c306b6c4ca1edf3138622336cbf3bec42e`.

[Model and results](https://github.com/GoGoKo699/Quantum-Sensing-Without-Bath-Resets/blob/b32469c306b6c4ca1edf3138622336cbf3bec42e/science/model_and_results.md).

The result guarantees local inverse-time risk for a probe coupled to a finite inaccessible persistent environment. Its general construction increases correction rate with the budget; its fixed-cadence subclass is a separate statement.
